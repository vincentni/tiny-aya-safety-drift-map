Tiny Aya Global gives a harmful answer to 1 in 6 harmful requests in Russian and 1 in 50 in French. A $0.49 benign fine-tune did not make it worse.

Tiny Aya Global (3.35B) on 600 red-teaming prompts in six languages, before and after a benign multilingual LoRA fine-tune. One judge labels every answer under a fixed public rubric. Code and write-up.

Content warning. The study uses red-teaming prompts. This page shows counts only: no prompt text and no model answer.

The fine-tune costs no safety, mostly because its answers are shorter and it refuses slightly more; among complete answers it is not safer.
