Where a small multilingual model's safety is thin, and what a $0.49 fine-tune does about it

Tiny Aya Global (3.35B) on 600 red-teaming prompts in six languages, before and after a benign multilingual LoRA fine-tune. One judge labels every answer under a fixed public rubric. Code and write-up.

Content warning. The study uses red-teaming prompts. This page shows prompt ids, languages, harm categories and the judge's labels. It shows no prompt text and no model answer.

The fine-tuned model also answers much more briefly, and the drop in harmful answers travels with that. The write-up says what the comparison does and does not show.
